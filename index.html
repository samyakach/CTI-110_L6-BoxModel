What is a Sea Dragon?

The Sea Dragon leviathan is a leviathan class organism in the Subnautica universe. It is native to the extremely deep and hot volcanic biomes inside the volcanic crater on planet 4546b, specifically the Inactive and Active Lava Zones. Only 3 are still alive within the timeframe of Subnautica, two in the ILZ and one in the lava lakes, acting as a sort of guardian to the Primary Containment facility. Reaching 112 meters in length, Sea Dragons are the second largest (non-extinct) fauna on 4546b, and serve as the Apex predator in their ecosystem.

One of the most notable behaviors of Sea Dragons is how they hunt for food. They are known to ascend to shallower waters to hunt down Reper Levaithans and drag them down to the lava zones, where they're boiled alive. (This is evident by the Reaper skeletons littered throughout the Inactive Lava Zone.)

Features of the Sea Dragon

Due to the flowing lava in their natural habitat, the water temperatures can exceed 170°F, causing the sea dragons to gain quite a few adaptations that make them suited to live in such environments. Sea Dragons' skin is covered in extremely thick and heat-resistant scales. Moreover, they're known for their one-of-a kind behavior/attack. Sea Dragons occasionally consume molten rock from their environment and spit it out at their prey to inflict massive damage. (This is the only attack in Subnautica that can defeat the player in one hit.)

The Roar

Another notable characteristic of Sea Dragons is their iconic roar, which is impossible to miss whenever you're in the Lava Lakes or ILZ. To create this guttural roar, the developers actually combined the real life bellow of a bull, elephant, and tiger.
